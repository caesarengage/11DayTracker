Standing rules

Electrolytes daily: salt food properly, magnesium at night, 3L+ water. Fasting in Kuwaiti August without this is how sprints end early.

Craving protocol: cravings crest and pass in ~15 minutes. Sparkling water with lime, leave the room, message someone, or pick up the guitar. Never negotiate at hour zero of an unplanned evening — that's why evenings are set by noon.

If you slip on food, you have not slipped on sobriety. The KRs are independent. A bad meal is a data point; the streak stays intact and is the one thing that never resets by choice.

Body warning signs — shakiness, heavy sweating, racing heart, dizziness in days 1–4: eat, hydrate, and see a doctor same day. No plan outranks that.
